What Healthcare Revenue Group does

HRG runs two service lines that clients use independently or together. About half of our credentialing clients have no billing relationship with us, and about a third of our billing clients started with credentialing. Neither service is a gateway to the other.

In both, our specialists work inside the client's own systems and payer portals, so a practice administrator sees the same claim, note, and status that we see. Clients deal directly with the people doing the work, and there is no offshore team.

Medical billing and revenue cycle management

Billing for physician practices and federally qualified health centers, covering claim submission, scrubbing claims before they go out so fewer come back denied, appeal management when one does, and accounts receivable follow-up and recovery. The billers assigned to each account run its weekly and monthly A/R reviews themselves, and the practice has direct access to them.

We bill for clients on a set of supported EHR and practice management systems, and a short call confirms whether a practice's system is one of them.

Credentialing, contracting, and enrollment

Initial credentialing and recredentialing with deadline tracking, CAQH profile setup and attestation, NPI registration, primary source verification, DEA and state license assistance, multi-state licensing, and hospital privileges and reappointments, where HRG holds a 100 percent approval record.

Contracting covers initial payer contracts, network participation analysis, reimbursement rate negotiation and renegotiation, contract review, and fee schedule management. Enrollment covers Medicare, Medicaid, and managed care organizations, and for FQHCs we add HRSA audit readiness, FTCA compliance support, and Medicaid wraparound strategy.

Once we have what we need from the provider, our side of a credentialing file is typically done in 5 to 10 days. From there the payers set the pace. Medicare may run approximately 30 to 45 days for a clean application, Medicaid and managed care timelines vary by state and plan and may take longer, and commercial carriers generally run 90 to 120 days. We file clean, follow up weekly, and escalate applications that stall, and we never quote a payer's timeline as a guarantee.

Who we work with

Practices from 2 to more than 100 providers in billing-complex specialties, including cardiology, dermatology, podiatry, rheumatology, urgent care, behavioral health, chiropractic, primary care, pain management, and ENT, along with federally qualified health centers, multi-state telehealth groups, and locum tenens agencies. Clients often come to us after a billing vendor underperformed, an in-house biller left mid cycle, new providers sat unbillable while enrollment dragged, CAQH attestations lapsed, or a closed payer panel blocked growth.

What we do not do

  • We do not perform coding. We provide coding consultation as needed, and coders sit on the client side.
  • We do not manage prior authorizations. We advise on workflow, and the process stays with the practice.
  • We do not use offshore teams.
  • We do not work in a separate dashboard or a third-party credentialing platform.
  • We are not NCQA accredited, so a procurement process that strictly requires NCQA accreditation may not be a fit.
  • We do not guarantee payer decisions or payer timelines.
